# Which modules fit you

There is no plan to choose and no tier to move up. You start on the free core
and buy **only the capability you're missing**. There is no wrong choice to get
locked into: you can add a module later and pay pro rata for the days left
until your anniversary.

Buying and paying requires the **Admin (ADMIN)** or **Manager (MANAGER)** role
in your business; cancelling a product is **Admin (ADMIN) only**.

## Ask this first: is the core not enough?

POS, kitchen display, menu, floor plan, QR menu, orders, cash, basic reports,
team management and customers are **already free and unlimited**. You only pay
for a module once one of the needs below appears.

| Your need | What to buy | Yearly |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Recipes/costing, stock counts, purchase orders, waste tracking | Inventory & Cost Management | ₺3,900 |
| Deeper analytics, the accounting back-office, e-document settings | Advanced Reports & Analytics | ₺1,290 |
| Table reservations, a public booking page | Reservation System | ₺990 |
| Attendance, shift planning, performance | Staff Management | ₺990 |
| Yemeksepeti / Getir / Trendyol orders reaching the kitchen | The matching delivery integration | ₺2,490 (each) |
| Legally filed e-Fatura / e-Arşiv | e-Invoice (Nilvera) | **included in Maintenance, Support & Updates** |
| Fiscal receipts from a cash register | Fiscal printer (Hugin) | ₺2,990 |
| A kitchen that takes phone orders | Caller-ID Integration | ₺1,490 |
| SMS notifications to customers | SMS Notifications | ₺990 |
| Generating product images/video with AI | AI Menu Studio | ₺1,990 |
| Connecting your own systems | API & Webhook Access | ₺2,490 |
| A third-party screen showing your menu | Partner Display API | ₺1,990 |
| A guaranteed response time | Priority Support | **included in Maintenance, Support & Updates** |
| A second (or further) branch | Extra Branch | ₺3,990 / unit |

All of these require the **licence (₺4,900/yr)**: your first paid item brings the
licence onto the bill with it.

## Example baskets

The amounts below are first-year totals when everything is bought **at the same
time** (so every line is at full annual price). All are VAT-inclusive.

| Profile | Basket | First year |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| A small café that wants to see its costs | Licence + Inventory & Cost | **₺4,480** |
| A city-centre restaurant with bookings and shifts | Licence + Reservation + Staff + Advanced Reports | **₺6,260** |
| A delivery-heavy operation | Licence + Yemeksepeti + Getir + Caller-ID | **₺9,460** |
| A two-branch business | Licence + Extra Branch + Inventory & Cost + Advanced Reports | **₺9,760** |

<Callout type="info">
  None of these baskets is a fixed bundle; every line can be bought on its own
  and dropped on its own. You never pay for anything that isn't on your list.
</Callout>

## The purchase flow

<Steps>

### Open the store

Go to the **Store** (`/admin/store`) from the store icon in the top bar. The
catalogue is grouped into licence, modules, integrations, capacity, credits and
services.

### Tick what you need

You can tick several items at once; the **bill** panel updates the total as you
go. If you tick a paid module without holding a licence, the licence is added to
the bill automatically.

### Review the amount

Each line shows both the **prorated amount you pay now** and the **full renewal
amount on your anniversary**. The total is always recomputed on the server; what
you see on screen is what you'll be charged (VAT included).

### Accept the legal documents

Before checkout starts you're asked to accept the distance-selling documents.
Payment cannot begin until they are accepted.

### Pay with PayTR

Payment is taken through **PayTR** and in **TRY** only. Every item in your
basket appears as its own line on the payment screen.

### Access opens

The moment the payment is confirmed, what you bought is added to your account
and the matching screens become usable. If you abandon the payment, nothing is
charged and nothing is added.

</Steps>

## Adding a module later

You can add a module whenever you like. The new item is priced for the **days
left until your anniversary**, so your account keeps meeting on a single renewal
date.

Example: you bought the licence on **1 March**, so your anniversary is 1 March
every year. If you add the ₺3,900 Inventory & Cost module on **1 September**,
181 days remain, so you pay **₺1,933.97** this year; the module renews at full
price on 1 March.

<Callout type="info">
  If **14 days or fewer** remain until your anniversary, the item isn't squeezed
  into that stub: the remaining days plus one whole next cycle are billed
  together and the product stays open until the following anniversary. Details:
  [Billing & renewal](/en/plans/trial-and-billing).
</Callout>

## You won't be sold something you already have

The store won't let you tick an unbuyable item, and checkout re-runs the same
check:

- A product that is **already active** on your account is not sold again.
- If a capability was **granted to you** (complimentary) or is already opened by
  another product, the product that grants the same thing is not sold again.
- A capacity item that raises a limit which is already unlimited is not sold.
- While your licence is active, a second licence is not sold; the licence renews
  on your anniversary.
- **Extra Branch** is the exception: it is quantity-based, so you can buy a
  second and a third unit (up to 100 per account).

## Dropping a module

- **Cancel at period end** — the product stays open until the end of the period
  you paid for and doesn't join the renewal basket on your anniversary.
- **Cancel immediately** — the product's access is revoked right away.

Cancelling is a billing decision and is **Admin (ADMIN) only**. For
quantity-based items like **Extra Branch**, reducing the quantity also takes
effect at the next renewal — you keep your current quantity until the period you
paid for ends.
