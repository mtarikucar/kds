# Feature matrix

There are no plans to compare. There is only one question: **is this capability
in the free core, or which paid module unlocks it?** The tables below give the
full list.

## Limits

The infinity sign (**∞**) means that limit is **unlimited**.

| Limit | Free core | How it grows |
| --- | :---: | --- |
| Users | ∞ | — |
| Tables | ∞ | — |
| Products | ∞ | — |
| Categories | ∞ | — |
| Monthly orders | ∞ | — |
| Branches | 1 | Each **Extra Branch** (₺3,990/yr per unit) raises your branch allowance by one; up to 100 per account |

Branches are the only priced capacity item. The branch panel and the branch
switcher themselves are free — what you pay for is **the second and every
further branch**.

## Capabilities that run on credits

Two capabilities consume **credits** per use once they are open:

- **AI Menu Studio** — image, video and 3D model generations draw down credits.
  Packs: 100 images ₺690 · 20 videos ₺890 · 10 3D models ₺790.
- **SMS Notifications** — every send draws down credits. 500 SMS ₺490.

Credits are one-time, never expire and are valid until consumed; they are not
part of the annual renewal. To buy credits you must own the module or
integration they belong to.

## Which capabilities do I have right now?

The **Licence & Access** page (`/admin/license`) lists the products you own, the
end date of each and what it will cost at renewal; your credit balances are on
the same page.

What you see in the panel is your **actual access**: it is computed by combining
the products you bought, any capabilities granted to you specifically
(complimentary), and the free core.

<Callout type="warning">
  While your licence is inactive, licence-gated products grant nothing — the
  product row stays in your list but the capability reads as off. Renewing the
  licence re-lights all of them exactly as they were.
</Callout>
