# Billing & renewal

The free core is never invoiced and never charged. This page is only about the
**paid items**: the licence, modules, integrations, capacity, credits and
services.

## The anniversary: your account's one date

The day you buy the licence becomes your account's **immutable anniversary**. It
never drifts — not even if you pay a renewal late.

Its only purpose is this: **one renewal date, one invoice.** Whatever you buy
during the year lines up on that same day, so you never track a separate date
per product.

## Prorated pricing

An annual item bought during the year is priced for the **days left until your
anniversary**.

**Example.** You bought the licence on 1 March, so your anniversary is 1 March
every year. If you add the ₺3,900 Inventory & Cost module on 1 September:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Days left to the anniversary | 181 (in a 365-day cycle) |
| What you pay now | **₺1,933.97** (3,900 × 181 / 365) |
| Renewal on 1 March | ₺3,900 (full year) |

<Callout type="info">
  **If 14 days or fewer remain** until your anniversary, the item isn't squeezed
  into that stub: the amount for the remaining days **plus one whole next cycle**
  is billed together and the product stays open until the following anniversary.
  For instance, the ₺990 Reservation module bought 10 days out costs
  ₺27.12 + ₺990 = **₺1,017.12**.
</Callout>

Three more pricing rules:

- **No line ever falls below ₺1.** If proration produces a smaller amount, the
  line is rounded up to ₺1.
- **A renewal is always full price.** An item you paid pro rata for during the
  year renews at the full annual list price on your anniversary.
- **One-time items are never prorated.** Credit packs and services such as
  on-site installation are flat amounts and don't join the renewal.

## VAT-inclusive pricing

All advertised prices are **gross** (VAT-inclusive) amounts, and you are charged
exactly that amount — no extra VAT is added on top at invoice time. On the
invoice the total is broken down so the VAT it contains appears as its own line.

**Example — Licence ₺4,900:**

| Line | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Subtotal (net) | ₺2,491.67 |
| VAT (20%) | ₺498.33 |
| **Total (charged)** | **₺4,900.00** |

<Callout type="info">
  Your business's tax ID is written onto the invoice **as it was at the time the
  invoice was issued**. Even if you change your tax ID later, your past invoices
  are unaffected.
</Callout>

## Payment

Payment is collected through **PayTR** and in **TRY** only. Every item in your
basket appears as its own line on the payment screen, and you're asked to accept
the distance-selling documents before checkout starts.

Buying and paying requires the **Admin (ADMIN)** or **Manager (MANAGER)** role.

<Callout type="warning">
  If a payment is abandoned, nothing is charged and nothing is added. A
  capability only reaches your account once the payment is confirmed.
</Callout>

## Renewal is manual

**There is no automatic card charge and no card is kept on file.** Once a year,
you renew by paying. The flow works like this:

<Steps>

### The renewal basket is prepared

About **30 days** before your anniversary, a renewal basket is built from the
annual products you hold at that moment and its total is **frozen**. Even if the
catalogue price changes afterwards, you pay the amount you were quoted.

### Reminders arrive

Reminders are sent **30, 7 and 1 day** before the anniversary. The amount and
date also appear on the renewal card on your **Licence & Access** page.

### You pay

The link on the renewal card settles the whole basket in a single payment. Your
products stay open until the next anniversary.

### If you don't: a 7-day grace period

You get **7 days of grace** after the anniversary. Everything stays open during
that window and you can still renew by paying.

### If grace runs out: access goes dark

Paid products that weren't renewed switch off. If the licence lapses, access to
every licence-gated module goes with it.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Your data is not deleted.** When access goes dark, no business record is
  touched — your stock items, reservations, personnel records, generated AI
  media, invoices and orders all stay exactly where they are. Paying re-lights
  everything as it was.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  The free core is unaffected by any of this: POS, kitchen display, menu, floor
  plan, QR menu, orders, cash and team management keep running.
</Callout>

## Seeing your invoices

Your invoices are listed at the bottom of the **Licence & Access** page
(`/admin/license`): invoice number, date, line count and total. Every invoice is
itemized, so you can see exactly what you paid for each product.

The same page also shows your licence status and renewal date, the list of
products you own (with each one's end date and renewal amount), and your credit
balances.

## How credits are billed

Credit packs are bought **once**, **never expire** and are **valid until
consumed**. They don't join the renewal basket; when a balance runs out you buy
another pack. To buy credits you must own the product they belong to (AI credits
require AI Menu Studio, SMS credits require SMS Notifications).
