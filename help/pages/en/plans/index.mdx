# Licence & modules

The HummyTummy core is **free and stays open indefinitely**. There are no plans,
no tiers, no packages and no trial period. You buy the paid modules you need
**one at a time**, annually — and you never pay for anything you don't use.

All prices are in **Turkish Lira (TRY)** and are **VAT-inclusive**; the amount
you see on screen is the amount you'll be charged.

## The free core

The following is open on every business **indefinitely**, with **no credit card**
and **no licence required**:

- **POS and tabs** — order taking and the sales screen
- **Kitchen display (KDS)**
- **Menu management**
- **Tables and floor plan**
- **QR menu**
- **Order management**
- **Cash drawer and cash handling**
- **Basic reports**
- **Team and role management**
- **Customers**
- **Device and branch panel**
- **Custom branding and subdomain**

Usage limits are part of the core too:

- **Users, tables, products, categories and monthly orders are unlimited.**
- **Your first branch is free.** The branch panel itself is free as well; the
  only priced capacity item is **the second and every further branch**.

<Callout type="info">
  You don't have to enter payment details to start using the core, and there is
  no trial clock to run out. If you want to look around before setting anything
  up with your own data, you can switch into the **shared demo restaurant** from
  the panel and browse it with real data.
</Callout>

## What the licence is for

| Product | Yearly |
| --- | --- |
| **HummyTummy Licence** | **₺2,990** |

The licence is the prerequisite for **both buying and using** any paid module.
If you don't hold one when you pick a module, the store adds the licence to your
bill automatically.

The day you buy the licence becomes your account's **immutable anniversary**:
every annual item you add later is priced pro rata up to that date, so your
account renews on one date with one invoice. Details:
[Billing & renewal](/en/plans/trial-and-billing).

<Callout type="warning">
  If the licence goes dark (isn't renewed), **your paid modules go dark with
  it** — licence-gated products grant nothing while the licence is inactive.
  Your data is not deleted; paying re-lights everything exactly as it was.
</Callout>

## Where this lives in the panel

- **Licence & Access** (`/admin/license`, sidebar *Settings & Access → Plan &
  Access*) — your licence status, the products you own, your credit balances,
  your renewal date and your invoices.
- **Store** (`/admin/store`, the store icon in the top bar) — tick the items you
  want from the catalogue, watch the total update, and buy them all in one
  payment.
