# Dashboard

**Menu path:** Sidebar (top) → Dashboard &nbsp;•&nbsp; **URL:** `/dashboard`
**Access:** All roles (content varies by role)  •  **Requires:** Included in the free core

## Purpose

The Dashboard is the **landing screen** you see after signing in. It brings three things together at a glance: a fast jump to the **primary action** for your role, **shortcuts** to the most-used screens, and your business's **usage meters**. The goal is to make it clear where to start your day and to keep your volume visible.

## What's on the screen

- **Primary action card (hero):** The main job highlighted for your role. For cashier/waiter roles this is usually **POS**; for the kitchen role it's the **Kitchen Display (KDS)**.
- **Quick shortcuts:** One-tap access to frequently used screens such as Tables, Users, Customers, QR Codes, Reports and Licence/Settings.
- **Usage meters:** "used / limit" indicators for **Users**, **Branches**, **Products** and **Monthly orders**.

<Callout type="info">
  Users, products, categories, tables and **monthly orders** are **unlimited** in the
  free core — those meters show **∞** as the limit and only help you watch your volume.
  The one item with a real cap is the **branch count**: the first branch is free, and
  each further branch is added with an **Extra Branch** item (₺3,990/year each) — see
  [Plans](/en/plans).
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  The dashboard looks **different** by role: restricted roles such as KITCHEN/COURIER only
  see their relevant shortcuts and primary action. If a card doesn't appear, that screen
  isn't open to your role or you don't own the paid module behind it — see
  [Users & Roles](/en/admin-guide/users).
</Callout>
