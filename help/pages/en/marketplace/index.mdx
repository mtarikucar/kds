# Marketplace

**Menu path:** Side menu → Marketplace &nbsp;•&nbsp; **URL:** `/admin/marketplace`
**Access:** ADMIN, MANAGER (purchase confirmation is ADMIN-only) • **Module required:** None — the Marketplace is part of the free core

The Marketplace is the catalogue of items you can buy **individually** on top of
the free core. The core (POS, KDS, menu, tables, QR menu, cash, basic reports,
team management) is free forever; here you buy only the **module**,
**integration**, **capacity**, **credit pack**, or **service** you actually need.

Each card shows what the item does, its price (₺), and whether it is annual or
one-time. When you buy, the entitlement it grants (for example one extra branch
or a new integration) is **added to your account automatically** — the related
feature becomes usable in the panel right away.

<Callout type="warning">
  The **Maintenance, Support & Updates** is the prerequisite for every paid module: you need
  it both to buy one and to use it. Add a module to your cart without a licence
  and the screen asks you to add the licence first (`LICENSE_REQUIRED`). Credit
  packs and on-site setup need no licence; AI credits do depend on **AI Menu
  Studio** and SMS credits on **SMS Notifications**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  All prices are in **TRY** and shown **KDV-inclusive**. Payments are collected
  through PayTR. Annual items are priced pro rata for the days left until your
  anniversary — see [Purchase Flow](/en/marketplace/purchase-flow).
</Callout>
