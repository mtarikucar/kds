# Products (Catalogue)

**Menu path:** Side menu → Marketplace &nbsp;•&nbsp; **URL:** `/admin/marketplace`
**Access:** ADMIN, MANAGER • **Module required:** None — the Marketplace is part of the free core

Every item you can buy in the Marketplace is documented below, one by one. Each
card lists: **what it does**, **price**, **annual vs one-time**, and any
**dependency**. The core (POS, KDS, menu, tables, QR menu, cash, basic reports,
team management) is already free forever and is not sold here.

<Callout type="info">
  The purchase path is the same for every item: add it to the cart in the
  Marketplace → pay via PayTR → the entitlement activates automatically. For the
  step-by-step walkthrough see [Purchase Flow](/en/marketplace/purchase-flow).
  Prices are TRY, KDV-inclusive; annual items cover one licence cycle (a year)
  and are priced pro rata for the days left until your anniversary when bought
  mid-year.
</Callout>

---

## Licence

### HummyTummy Licence

The annual licence required to **buy and use** any paid module. The day you buy
it becomes your account's fixed **anniversary**; every annual item bought later
is priced pro rata against that date.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | Licence |
| Billing | Annual |
| Price | ₺2,990.00 |
| What it does | The prerequisite for every paid module, integration and capacity item |
| Dependency | — |

<Callout type="warning">
  The licence is not just a purchase gate: when the licence term lapses, the
  grants of the modules that depend on it are suppressed too. The free core is
  unaffected and your data is never deleted.
</Callout>
