# Purchase Flow

**Menu path:** Side menu → Marketplace &nbsp;•&nbsp; **URL:** `/admin/marketplace`
**Access:** ADMIN, MANAGER (payment confirmation and cancellation are ADMIN-only) • **Module required:** None — the Marketplace is part of the free core

The only way to buy an item is to add it to the cart in the Marketplace and
**pay via PayTR**. No entitlement (limit or feature) is added to your account
until the payment completes; there is no way to activate a paid item for free.
The free core needs no purchase at all — it is already on, forever.

<Callout type="info">
  Operator comps can only be issued by the HummyTummy team / superadmin. On your
  side, the only way to buy is the paid flow.
</Callout>

## End-to-end flow

<Steps>

### Pick an item

Find the item you want to buy on the Marketplace screen. Each card shows what it
does, its price (₺), and whether it is annual or one-time.

### Add to cart

Add the item to the cart. You can put several items in the same cart: an opening
cart of **licence + module(s) + credit pack** settles cleanly, because the server
provisions in dependency order. The screen shows the total **KDV-inclusive**, and
the total is always recalculated on the server, so the amount you pay is
trustworthy.

### Continue to payment

Use the **Pay / Continue** button to move to the payment step. The screen
redirects you to the **PayTR** secure payment page, where each item in your cart
appears as its own line (e.g. *"Inventory & Cost Management (annual)"*). Payment
is collected in **TRY** only.

### Complete the payment

Pay on the PayTR page. Once the payment is confirmed, you are returned to the app.

### Entitlements activate

The moment the payment is confirmed, the entitlement the item grants is **added
to your account automatically**. For example, if you bought one Extra Branch,
your branch limit goes up by one in the panel right away; if you bought a module
or an integration, the related screen/setting is enabled; if you bought a credit
pack, your balance is topped up.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  If you reach the payment page but abandon the payment, you are not charged and
  no entitlement is added. The purchase only completes once the payment is
  confirmed.
</Callout>

## How entitlements stack

What you buy stacks on top of the free core; multiple sources of the same kind
combine like this:

- **Limits** (branches) → **summed.** The core gives 1 branch; buy 2 "Extra
  Branch" units and the total becomes **3**.
- **Features** (advanced reports, priority support, reservations, etc.) →
  **enabled** if any source enables it.
- **Integrations** (e-Invoice, Yemeksepeti, Getir, Trendyol) → all accumulate
  side by side; you can use several delivery integrations at once.
- **Credits** → accumulate until consumed; a second pack adds to what is left of
  the first.

Paying twice for the same module or integration is blocked: if it is already
active, the purchase is rejected (`ADDON_ALREADY_GRANTED` / `ADDON_ALREADY_OWNED`)
and your card is not charged again. **Extra Branch** is the exception — it is
quantity-based and can be bought repeatedly, up to 100 branches per account.

## Dependencies

- The **HummyTummy Licence** is the prerequisite for every paid module,
  integration and capacity item. Without one the purchase is blocked
  (`LICENSE_REQUIRED`) — unless the licence is in **the same cart**, which
  satisfies it.
- **Credit packs** require the item they belong to: AI credits require **AI Menu
  Studio**, SMS credits require **SMS Notifications**. A sibling cart line
  satisfies this too.
- Credit packs and **On-site Installation & Training** need no licence.

## Renewal

Billing runs off your anniversary: the day you buy the licence becomes your
account's **fixed anniversary**, and every annual item is collected onto **one
invoice with one renewal date**. An item bought mid-year is priced pro rata for
the days left until that anniversary; if fewer than 14 days remain, the remainder
is billed together with the next full cycle. No line is ever priced below ₺1.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Renewal is manual.** There is no automatic card charge and no card on file.
  You get reminders **30, 7 and 1 day** before the renewal date, and a **7-day
  grace period** after it. If it goes unpaid, only **access to the paid items
  goes dark** — **no data is deleted**, and everything comes back exactly as it
  was the moment you pay. The free core is never affected.
</Callout>

## Cancellation behaviour

Only an **ADMIN** can cancel an item (it's a billing decision). You have two
options:

- **Cancel at period end (default):** the item **stays active until the end of
  the period**, and the entitlements it grants keep working until that date. When
  the period ends it closes automatically and the related limit/feature is
  revoked.
- **Cancel immediately:** the item closes at once and the entitlements it grants
  are **revoked right away**.

<Callout type="info">
  Annual items run on a period aligned to your licence anniversary. One-time
  lines (credit packs, on-site setup) have no period to cancel; credits stay with
  you until consumed.
</Callout>
